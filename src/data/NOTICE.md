# Third-party material

Pellaeon's own code and writing are under the MIT license in `LICENSE`. That license does not extend to the material below, which stays with its owners.

- **UCSF ChimeraX and UCSF Chimera documentation.** `src/data/cheatsheet.json`, `src/data/tutorials.json` and `src/data/guides/chimerax_cli_guide_deep.txt` are summaries and adaptations of the ChimeraX documentation. In the Classic edition, `classic/pellaeon_classic/data/chimera_docs.json.gz` holds excerpts of the Chimera User's Guide, and its other data files adapt the same guide. Copyright The Regents of the University of California.
- **ChimeraX recipes.** `src/data/recipe_library.json` summarises recipes published by the Resource for Biocomputing, Visualization, and Informatics (RBVI) at UCSF, with the commands they show.

UCSF ChimeraX and UCSF Chimera are developed by the RBVI at the University of California, San Francisco, and neither is included in Pellaeon. Pellaeon is not affiliated with or endorsed by UCSF.
